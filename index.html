Two dials decide how quickly you use up your weekly Claude limit: which model you pick, and how hard it thinks. Try a combination.

Dial 1: the model

The model is which brain you pick. Bigger brains handle harder work and cost more for every token they read or write.

Haiku 4.5Fastest, lightest

Quick, simple, repetitive jobs like renaming, tagging, and short rewrites.

Effort: No effort setting. It always runs light.

1x, cheapest
Sonnet 5.5The workhorse

Most design, writing, and coding tasks, especially well-defined ones.

Effort: Low to Max. Medium for well-defined work, High when it misses. Skip Max.

2x
Opus 5.5Careful thinker

Complex, multi-step problems where quality and judgment matter more than speed.

Effort: Low to Max. Medium is its default and a strong start. Extra gets close to Max for less.

4x
Fable 5.1Most capable

The hardest, longest jobs, like big agent runs across many files. Needs paid usage credits.

Effort: Low to Max. Try Low or Medium first. Max adds little but costs about three times as much as Low.

10x, use sparingly

The multipliers compare list price per token, with Haiku as 1x. Per task the gap is smaller, because bigger efforts make a model write more. See "Quality vs cost" below.

Dial 2: the effort

Effort is how long Claude thinks before it answers. More thinking gives more thorough answers, but slower replies and a faster-draining limit. It sits next to the model picker. Haiku 4.5 has no effort setting.

LowSimple, known jobs
MediumEveryday work. Start here
HighWhen Medium misses
ExtraWhen High misses
MaxOne really hard task

Rule of thumb: start on Medium and only go up after an answer misses. Taller bar means more thinking and more limit used. Check what your picker defaults to, because it differs between apps.

What else eats tokens

These drain your limit on any model, at any effort.

Long chats

Claude re-reads the whole conversation with every message. Start a fresh chat for each new task.

Big Figma selections and files

Point Claude at one frame, not the whole page, and attach only what the task needs.

Long answers

What Claude writes costs about 5x more than what you send. Ask for short answers when you can.

Why only these four models

The model picker lists a lot of old versions. Ignore them. Claude comes in four families, and you only need the newest version of each.

The older ones stay in the list for people who need an exact old version. For us there's no reason to pick them: the newer version of each family costs the same or less. Opus 5.5 is cheaper than Opus 5, and Fable 5.1 costs the same as Fable 5. If your picker only shows Sonnet 5, pick the newest Sonnet you have.
